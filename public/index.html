GROW YOUR COLLECTION

Bring your cards along

Import a CSV with name,set,card_number,quantity. The extracted Pokémon and Trainer files also accept Collector No. and Qty; Energy accepts Energy,Qty,Type. Use full set names (or TCGdex set IDs). A card_id,quantity file also works. Duplicate rows are combined.

Recognized Pokémon, Trainer, and Energy files replace only their own category; generic files replace the whole collection. A snapshot sets absent cards in that scope to zero. Changes that leave a saved deck short of cards are rejected. Sandstorm will ask you to connect TCGdex for details and faces. Faces are saved in this grain. Energy without a printing uses labeled representative artwork.
