Bring your cards along

Import a CSV with name,set,card_number,quantity. The extracted Pokémon and Trainer files also accept Collector No. and Qty; Energy accepts Energy,Qty,Type. Use set names, IDs, or printed codes such as MEW. You may omit the set when the name and printed number/total uniquely identify the card. A card_id,quantity file also works. Duplicate rows are combined.

Recognized Pokémon, Trainer, and Energy files replace only their own category; generic files replace the whole collection. A snapshot sets absent cards in that scope to zero. Changes that leave a saved deck short of cards are rejected. Sandstorm will ask you to connect TCGdex for details and faces. Faces are saved in this grain. Energy without a known printing uses a sample card image.

Add a card

Search TCGdex by name or card ID, then choose the exact printing. Use a set name or code (such as MEW), or leave the set blank and enter the name and printed number/total. If several printings match, browse their faces with the arrows.
