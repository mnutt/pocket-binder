Pocket Binder helps you organize a Pokémon card collection and build decks from the cards you own.

- Look up exact printings in TCGdex and preview card artwork before adding cards.
- Import and export your collection as CSV, or add cards individually.
- Browse your collection as a gallery and inspect cards in a full-height detail view.
- Build multiple decks, track available copies, and get casual deck-building guidance.
- Record card trades.
- Print compact collection and deck inventories.

Each Sandstorm grain keeps its own collection, decks, and cached artwork. Share a grain with viewers or editors using Sandstorm's sharing controls. Card lookups and artwork downloads use connections you grant through Sandstorm.

Card data and artwork are provided by [TCGdex](https://tcgdex.dev). Deck guidance is intended for casual play.
